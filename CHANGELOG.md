## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-05

Initial version, based on [MMM-Snow](https://github.com/MichMich/MMM-Snow) by Michael Teeuw.

- add season themes that switch automatically: flowers in spring, butterflies and fireflies in summer, leaves in fall and snow in winter
- add special occasions that take over on their dates: Halloween, Christmas and New Year's Eve; occasions can be changed, disabled or added with `occasions`
- add movements: `downwards`, `upwards`, `windy`, `around` and `long around`
- add emoji and image particles, with per-entry settings such as `weight`, `movement` and `size`
- add 4 sample images each for spring, summer and fall, and 3 snowflake images for winter
- add options `theme`, `seasonMode`, `hemisphere`, `density`, `opacity`, `updateInterval` and `seasons`
